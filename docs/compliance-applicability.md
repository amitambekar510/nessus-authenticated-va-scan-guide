# Applicable regulation and framework references

This is a **risk-based alignment reference** for vulnerability assessment and remediation evidence. None of these twelve is blanket proof of an authenticated Nessus scanning mandate. Verify applicability, current versions, sectoral obligations and auditor requirements.

| Reference | Responsible use in VA workflow | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| CERT-In Directions 2022 | Relevant logs and incident-response evidence | Incident reporting and log obligations differ from VA scheduling |
| SEBI CSCRF 2024 | Vulnerability management and governance for applicable SEBI-regulated entities | Verify entity category and amendments |
| RBI IT / Cyber directions | Risk assessment, IT controls and remediation for applicable RBI entities | Rules depend on regulated-entity class |
| DPDP Act 2023 | Safeguards and privacy for personal data used in scan evidence | Check currently commenced provisions and rules |
| NSE/BSE technology audit | Scoped VA records for relevant members | Exchange circular and audit scope govern |
| NPCI/UPI requirements | Security testing evidence for covered payment participants | Participant-specific conditions apply |
| SEBI PFUTP 2003 | Securities fraud context if relevant to investigated systems | **Not a VA scanning standard**; indirect only |
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | Information security risk treatment; Annex A 8.8 management of technical vulnerabilities | Requires ISMS scope and Statement of Applicability |
| ISO/IEC 27090 | **Identifier/title and relevance require verification** | Do not claim coverage until confirmed |
| MITRE ATT&CK | Threat-context enrichment for findings | Knowledge base, not regulation |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | AI governance if an AI assistant forms part of an in-scope AI management system | Conditional; not a VA mandate |
| NIST CSF 2.0 | Identify/Protect/Detect/Respond risk management and improvement | Voluntary unless adopted/required |

An organization should map **specific provisions**, documented owners, evidence, dates, exclusions and residual risks. Listing a framework is not compliance. Authoritative starting points: https://www.cert-in.org.in/ , https://www.sebi.gov.in/ , https://www.rbi.org.in/ , https://www.meity.gov.in/ , https://www.nseindia.com/ , https://www.bseindia.com/ , https://www.npci.org.in/ , https://www.iso.org/standards.html , https://attack.mitre.org/ , https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework .
